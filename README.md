# RAPIDO DATA ANALYTICS
## Project Objective
Build an end-to-end data analytics project using Python (Pandas), PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI on Rapido ride-booking data.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/VarshithDev/rapido_data_analytics/blob/main/rapido_data.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
### Revenue Analysis
- What is the total gross revenue generated?
- How many total bookings were completed?
- What is the average fare earned per kilometer?
- How much revenue was lost due to ride cancellations?
### Fleet Performance
- Which fleet segment contributes the highest revenue?
- Which service receives the highest number of bookings?
- What is the overall ride fulfillment rate?
### Demand Analysis
- During which hours does booking demand peak?
- How does booking volume vary throughout the day?
### Operational Efficiency & Time Congestion
- What is the average trip duration?
- What is the average trip distance?
- What is the average network speed across all rides?
- How many gridlock incidents occurred?
- How does network fulfillment change throughout the day?
- Which fleet segment maintains the highest average speed?
- During which hours are ride cancellations highest?
### Diagnostic Analysis & Predictive Analysis
- Which day type (Weekday/Weekend) contributes most to revenue loss?
- Which service category has the highest revenue loss?
- Which payment method is associated with the highest revenue loss?
- Which pickup locations generate the most revenue loss?
- Which destination areas contribute the most cancellations?
- What is the predicted trend of the selected KPI over the next 7 days?
- What is the expected confidence interval for the forecast?
- How can future operational planning be improved using forecasted trends?

## Project Insights
💰 Generated a **total gross revenue of ₹25M** from approximately 50K successful ride bookings, reflecting strong business performance.

🚖 **Bike services emerged as the highest revenue-contributing fleet**, indicating strong customer preference for economical transportation.

📈 Achieved an **overall ride fulfillment rate of 90%**, demonstrating efficient ride allocation and successful order completion.

❌ Identified **nearly ₹3M in revenue loss due to ride cancellations**, highlighting opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

⏰ Peak booking hours were identified through hourly demand analysis, enabling optimized driver allocation during high-demand periods.

🚦 Network speed and trip duration analysis revealed their direct impact on ride completion and customer experience.

🛣️ **Gridlock incident analysis** helped uncover traffic congestion patterns affecting operational performance and service quality.

📊 **Fleet-wise performance comparison** provided insights into revenue contribution, booking distribution, and operational efficiency across different ride categories.

🔍 **AI-powered Decomposition Tree analysis identified the root causes of revenue loss** by analyzing service type, payment method, pickup location, destination, and day type.

💳 Payment method analysis highlighted customer payment preferences and their influence on ride cancellations and revenue generation.

📅 **Time-series forecasting predicted business trends for the next seven days**, supporting proactive resource planning and operational decision-making.

🚀 Developed an end-to-end analytics pipeline using Python (Pandas), PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI, transforming raw ride-booking data into interactive business intelligence dashboards.

## 🎯 Final Conclusion
The Rapido Analytics Project demonstrates a complete end-to-end analytics workflow, beginning with data profiling and cleaning using Python (Pandas), followed by relational database design in PostgreSQL, SQL-based data analysis, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI. The project provides valuable insights into revenue, bookings, fleet performance, operational efficiency, cancellations, and future demand through descriptive, diagnostic, operational, and predictive analytics. By converting raw ride-booking data into meaningful business intelligence, the project highlights the power of data-driven decision-making and reflects practical expertise in modern analytics and data engineering workflows.
